Multi-Modal Risk Fusion Architecture

Traditional systems rely solely on rainfall thresholds. TerraWatch NER continuously synthesizes three independent streams of physical evidence into a unified hazard index:

  • Spatial Stream (w=0.50): Terrain slope, elevation, lithology, and distance to faults.
  • Temporal Stream (w=0.35): Real-time geotechnical sensors tracking ground water pore pressure and moisture velocity.
  • Visual Stream (w=0.15): Computer vision inspection of crowd-sourced hazard imagery.

Academic Demonstration Notice • IMDAI Course

This project is an academic multi-modal AI demonstration prototype designed for the Intelligent Model Design using AI (IMDAI) course, inspired by the landslide risk challenge across North Eastern India.

Data Provenance: The 32 station coordinates reflect genuine geography across all 8 NER states. Environmental sensor data and training sets are simulated research baselines to demonstrate machine learning model behavior, temporal trends, and explainability. It is not an operational disaster-management dispatch system.
